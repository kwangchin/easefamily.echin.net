What's inside

  • Shared lists and notes

    Shopping lists, chores and packing lists that update on everyone's devices. Personal notes for you, family notes for everyone.

  • Planner

    Calendar, reminders and birthdays, including Chinese lunar dates, with alerts for the whole family.

  • Trips

    Flights, hotels and activities in their local time zones, with documents available offline.

  • Money

    Accounts in any currency, transfers, budgets and recurring payments. Import bank CSVs and see spending by category.

  • Library

    PDFs, photos and links in shared folders. Save from Safari, Photos or Files with the share sheet.

  • Private by design

    No accounts to create and no EaseFamily servers. Your data stays on your devices and in your iCloud, shared only with people you invite.

EaseFamily Pro

A custom Today, advanced search, bulk edits, a duplicate finder, advanced reports, exports, list rules and themes. Monthly, yearly or a one-time purchase, shared with your Apple Family Sharing group. Everything your family creates stays available to everyone, with or without Pro.
