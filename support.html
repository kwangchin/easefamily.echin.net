Getting started

  1. Sign in to iCloud on your iPhone, iPad or Mac.
  2. Open EaseFamily → Families → Create Family.
  3. Choose Invite Family Member and send the link. Each person opens it on their own device.

Common questions

Why isn't something showing on my other device?

Changes sync through iCloud when both devices are online. Check that both are signed in to the same iCloud account (for your personal items) and have a connection. In EaseFamily, open More and tap the sync status row, below Settings, to see any error. A full iCloud storage stops syncing until space is freed.

The invitation link doesn't work.

Open the link on the device where you use EaseFamily, signed in to iCloud. If it still fails, ask the family owner to open Families → the family → Manage Sharing and send a new invitation.

"Changes to Review" appeared. What does it mean?

Two people changed the same name or note at the same time, before their devices synced. EaseFamily keeps both versions so nothing is lost. Choose the one to keep, or type a new one.

I deleted something by mistake.

Open Recently Deleted (at the bottom of each list, in Families, Notes, Trips, the Library and Money) and choose Restore. Items stay there for 30 days.

A file says "Not Downloaded".

It hasn't reached this device yet. Keep the app open on a connection and try again shortly.

How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Settings → EaseFamily Pro → See EaseFamily Pro → Restore Purchases, signed in with the Apple Account that bought it. Members of your Apple Family Sharing group get Pro too.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In EaseFamily, Settings → EaseFamily Pro → See EaseFamily Pro → Manage Subscription, or in your device's Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
