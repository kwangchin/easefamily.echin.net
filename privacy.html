Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

EaseFamily is built so that your family's information stays with you. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't have accounts, and we can't see what you put in the app.

Where your data is kept

  • Everything you create is saved on your devices first, so the app works offline.
  • It syncs through your own iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit. Personal items are in your private iCloud database.
  • A family you create is stored in your iCloud and shared only with the people you invite. A family someone else invites you to is stored in their iCloud.
  • iCloud storage and security are provided by Apple under Apple's privacy policy.

What we collect

Nothing. EaseFamily doesn't collect, sell or share personal data, doesn't use analytics or advertising, and doesn't track you across apps or websites.

Things that reach the internet

  • Sync and sharing go to Apple's iCloud servers, encrypted in transit.
  • Saved links: when you save a new link, the device you're using fetches that page once to read its title and icon. The site sees an ordinary request from your device. Other family members' devices don't contact the site.
  • Purchases of EaseFamily Pro are handled by Apple. The app checks your purchases with Apple's StoreKit on your device. We don't receive your payment details or purchase history.

On your device

  • Spotlight: titles of notes, events, reminders, birthdays, trips, files, links and people are added to your device's search index. Note text, amounts and health details are never included. You can turn this off in EaseFamily's Settings.
  • Widgets read a summary of the next seven days from a file the app keeps on your device.
  • Notifications are scheduled on each device; no notification content leaves the device.

Family members and children

Anyone you invite to a family can see that family's lists, notes, events, people, trips, money and files, and can edit them if you allow it. Child profiles are information that parents add about their children; the app isn't designed for children to use on their own, and we don't knowingly collect anything from them.

Deleting your data

  • Deleted items move to Recently Deleted and are removed permanently after 30 days, or straight away with Delete Now.
  • Deleting a family you own removes it for everyone; leaving a family removes it from your devices.
  • To remove everything, delete EaseFamily's data in your device's iCloud settings (Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Account Storage).

Changes and contact

We'll update this page if anything changes, with a new date at the top. Questions are welcome at chin@echin.net or through the support page. Use of the app is also covered by the Terms of Use.
